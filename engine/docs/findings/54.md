## Part B — C4 witnesses

C4 is a negative property. The suite cannot verify it.
It can falsify it. It can witness consequences that
would be present if C4 were violated. Taxonomy 33:
Observed witnesses of a property that cannot be
exhaustively observed. Passing does not establish C4.

Three cases, both live engines. None failed.

## Observation

C4 was written in 1.15.0 because a sweep found it
missing. It was the last criterion anyone would have
predicted was unstated: the out-of-band architecture
exists to satisfy it, and every document described
it. It then sat as the specification's central claim
for six versions with no evidence. The first
assertion is this one — after the criterion, after
the audit, after the admission rule, and after two
engines shipped.

The claim everyone was surest of is the one that
went longest unchecked.

C13 occupies that position now. The digest is on
the artefact and on every decision this engine
writes. The specification still records its
declared evidence as none. The corpus does not
observe it.
