# What the same means

Two targets agree when they produce the
same **observable outcome** for the same
governed operation. They do not agree
when their configuration files match.

The compiled artefact is allowed to
differ. It differs by construction.
One target strips at a bound another
target cannot state (16c). One target
loses an unassigned path to a more
specific route the other still sees
(41). Record sinks sit at different
deployment levels (45, 50). A third
target may lack a mechanism the first
two share. None of that is the
comparison.

The comparison is what a caller, a
backend, or a later reader of a record
can see: the status, the identifier,
whether a header and a body that name
one instruction agree, and whether a
record of the decision is present or
absent.

A difference in how that outcome was
produced is not a disagreement. A
difference in the outcome, that is not
on the permitted-divergence list, is a
failure.
